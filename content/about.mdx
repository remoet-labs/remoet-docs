# About

Remoet lets you track tech jobs from companies you love. The job catalogue is public at [www.remoet.dev/jobs](https://www.remoet.dev/jobs), and starring a company puts its new roles into your own feed. We expose all of it through an MCP server and a REST API, so your agents and your own tools can work with the data directly.

Remoet is free for job seekers.

The product lives at [www.remoet.dev](https://www.remoet.dev). Source for these docs is at [github.com/remoet-labs/remoet-docs](https://github.com/remoet-labs/remoet-docs).
